# little-explorers

![Preschool worksheets with a maze, stars to count, a dot-to-dot puzzle and colored pencils.](.docs/little-explorers-cover.png)

little-explorers is an open-source collection of 64 printable games and worksheets for children aged 3–5. The activities are ad-free and available in 23 languages, with English as the default.

**[Live demo on GitHub Pages](https://milanjankovec.github.io/little-explorers/?lang=en)**

## Choose, generate, and print

1. Choose an activity and a language. Use a mouse or touchscreen to operate the browser controls.
2. Use the **New…** button to generate a fresh worksheet.
3. Select **Print worksheet** or **Print games**, choose A4 in portrait orientation, and print or save as a PDF. Children complete the activities on paper.

## Activities

- **Mazes and paths** — Little mazes · Star hunt maze · Arrow maze · Tangled strings · Paths That Don’t Cross · Complete the Road · Shortest Delivery Route · Follow the pattern path · Train through tunnels
- **Drawing and shapes** — Pencil tracing paths · Trace the shapes · Connect the dots · Mirror drawing · Colour by Symbol · Cut-out Puzzles · Build with Shapes · Cut along the lines · Fold along the Lines · Mix the Colours · One Stroke
- **Counting and sizes** — Count and Circle the Number · Make That Many Dots · Find and Count · Big and Small · More, fewer or the same · Share fairly · Measure with blocks · Picture graph · Little shop · Dice race · Add or take away · Balance the scale
- **Logic and observation** — Complete the pattern · Which one doesn’t belong? · Sort into Baskets · Shadow Matching · What’s missing? · Cut-out matching cards · Find the identical picture · What goes together? · What happened first? · Picture dominoes · Picture sudoku · Find the teddy · Picture bingo · How do you feel? · Remember the Grid · Two Rules at Once · Dress for the Weather · Picture code · Float or sink? · View from Above · Touch hunt
- **Letters and words** — First letters · Rhymes · Clap the syllables · Make up a story · Listen and draw · Play the rhythm · Hear the difference · Build a word · Finish the sentence · Say the opposite · Word Under the Magnifier

## Use offline

Download and extract a ZIP of the repository, or clone it with Git. Open [index.html](index.html) from the extracted or cloned folder in a browser. Each activity embeds its own CSS, JavaScript, and SVG graphics. You can generate and print worksheets without installing anything or starting a web server.
